Finding Us

Location & How to Get There

Selphi Farm, near Musa Primary School, Kisaju — Isinya, Kajiado County.

Get Directions

Driving Directions

Coming by car? Here's the route from Kitengela.

  1. From Kitengela, head towards Isinya on the main road.
  2. Pass East African University and continue to KAG University.
  3. Turn left onto the murram road — you'll see Isinya Eggs on the side and Rubis opposite.
  4. Drive straight until you reach Musa Primary School.
  5. Turn left at the school and follow the road to the end.
  6. Take a right turn and drive down until you see the black gate on your left — that's Selphi Farm.

By Matatu & Bodaboda

Travelling by public transport? Here's the easiest route from town.

  1. From Railways in Nairobi, board a matatu to Kitengela.
  2. At Kitengela, take another matatu heading towards Isinya and alight at Rubis — just before Isinya, near Isinya Eggs.
  3. From Rubis, hop on a bodaboda to Selphi Farm, near Musa Primary School, Kisaju.
